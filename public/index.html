How it works

  1. 01

    Tell us about your household

    Enter your city or share your GPS location. Add your commute mode, one-way distance and cooking fuel. Check which household members are sensitive to air pollution.

  2. 02

    We fetch live air quality

    Open Meteo provides current AQI, PM2.5, PM10, NO₂ and O₃ for your coordinates, plus a 48-hour PM2.5 hourly forecast. Deterministic rules then classify the AQI band and find the cleanest 2-hour window.

  3. 03

    Safety rules gate the AI

    Hard safety constraints are built in code — no outdoor exercise above AQI 150, stay indoors above 300, mask and ventilation rules for sensitive households. These are injected into the Gemini prompt and the output is validated against them.

  4. 04

    Get your personalised plan

    Gemini writes a contextual daily routine, indoor air hacks and sustainable alternatives inside the safety limits. Impact numbers (CO₂, exposure, cigarette equivalents) are calculated by code and shown beside each recommendation.

AQI reference guide

The US AQI scale used by Open Meteo. All recommendations in Clear Day are based on this classification.

AQI Range Level Who is at risk Recommended action
0–50 Good None Enjoy outdoor activities freely.
51–100 Moderate Unusually sensitive people Consider reducing prolonged outdoor exertion if sensitive.
101–150 Unhealthy for sensitive groups Children, elderly, asthma, pregnancy Keep outdoor time short. Wear a mask outdoors.
151–200 Unhealthy Everyone No outdoor exercise. Sensitive groups stay indoors.
201–300 Very Unhealthy Everyone Avoid all outdoor activity. Keep windows shut.
301+ Hazardous Everyone — emergency level Stay indoors. Seal gaps. Seek medical advice if symptomatic.

Questions people ask

Where does the air data come from?

From Open Meteo's air quality service, which uses CAMS European air quality forecast models. Readings are cached on our server for up to ten minutes.

Does the AI decide what is safe?

No. Fixed rules written in code set all safety limits before the AI sees your request. If the AI output contains any forbidden advice (such as outdoor running above AQI 150), it is replaced with a rule-based fallback plan.

What is the cigarette-equivalent figure?

Berkeley Air research found that a 22 µg/m³ daily mean PM2.5 exposure is roughly equivalent to smoking one cigarette per day. We use this ratio to make the health cost of today's air tangible and relatable.

What do you store about me?

Your profile is saved only in your browser's localStorage. It is sent to our server to build your plan but is never persisted there.

How are CO₂ and exposure numbers calculated?

Our code uses published emission factors (scooter 45 g/km, car 170 g/km, bus 70 g/km, metro 30 g/km) and a simple PM2.5 inhalation exposure model. All assumptions are listed under every plan.

What if the AI service is down?

You still get a complete plan built entirely from the deterministic safety rules. It is clearly labelled as a rule-based fallback, not AI-generated advice.

Is this medical advice?

No. Clear Day provides general air quality guidance. If you or a family member have a diagnosed respiratory condition, follow your doctor's advice.
